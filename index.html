About

From service management to shipping the tools myself

I work in IT service management, so I know the problems these tools are meant to solve: unclear ownership, work getting dropped during hand-offs, and managers who can't see what is overdue. I stopped waiting for someone else to build the fix and built it myself.

I like the whole front-end job: getting the layout and visual details right, keeping the interface fast and accessible, and getting state to behave when several people are editing at once. I learn by shipping real tools to real users and fixing what breaks.
